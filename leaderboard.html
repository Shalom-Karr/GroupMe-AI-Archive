ℹ How the leaderboard works

Every member's score starts at zero, and then:

  • Messages — you earn 1 point for every message you send in the group.
  • Likes received — you earn 20 points for every like your messages receive from other members.
  • Likes given — you earn 10 points for every like you give on someone else's message.
  • Leaving — you lose 25 points every time you leave the group on your own.
  • Getting kicked — you lose 500 points every time someone removes you from the group.
  • Deleted messages — you lose 5 points for every message of yours that gets deleted.
  • Inactive months — you lose 350 points for every calendar month in which you posted nothing, counted starting from the month you joined all the way through the current month. (Months where you sent at least one message cost nothing.)
Points = (messages × 1) + (likes received × 20) + (likes given × 10) − (leaves × 25) − (kicks × 500) − (deleted messages × 5) − (inactive months × 350)

Members with a negative total don't appear on the leaderboard.
